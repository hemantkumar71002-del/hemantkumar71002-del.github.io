About me

I am an electronics engineer. I like the place where hardware meets software: circuits and sensors on one side, apps and AI on the other. I learn by building things I can test on real devices, from sensor boards to Android apps.

My main areas are embedded systems, IoT, PCB design and AI that runs on the device itself. My projects are made to be useful for real people: Nancy AI for blind users, and Room Drive for teams and organisations.

Projects

Nancy AI

An Android app made for blind and low-vision people. The camera detects objects and speaks their direction, name and distance. It recognises familiar faces, so you know who is in front of you. You control everything by voice, so no screen is needed, and it works offline. It is a helping tool and does not replace a white cane or a guide.

Room Drive

An Android app for sharing files inside a company, school or team. The admin creates a room and gives each person a user ID and password. Users sign in with that ID, open PDF, Word, PowerPoint and image files, and upload files if the admin allows it. One user ID works on one device at a time, the admin can remove any ID at any moment, and new files appear live on every connected device.
